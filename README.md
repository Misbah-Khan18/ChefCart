# ChefCart 🥕
### Your recipes, your groceries, all in one place.

Ever saved a recipe and then realised you had no idea what ingredients you were missing?

ChefCart is my take on making that experience easier. It's a quick-commerce-inspired platform that connects recipe discovery with smart grocery shopping.

Find something you want to cook, adjust the servings, check what's already in your pantry, and add only the missing ingredients to your cart.

### What I'm building
- Recipe discovery and YouTube recipe importing
- AI-powered ingredient understanding
- Smart serving-size calculator
- Digital pantry management
- Grocery product matching and smart cart
- Estimated shopping costs
- AI-powered cooking assistance

### Tech Stack
React.js • Node.js • Express.js • PostgreSQL • AI APIs

### Current Status
Currently in development. Building the backend and core features step by step.

### The idea?
Less time figuring out what to buy, more time enjoying what you cook.
